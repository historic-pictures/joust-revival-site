Our mission

The joust, as it was

Why we are making Joust Revival, where it is going, and what we will and will not do on the way.

Draft: owner to review. This page will change as the owner writes more about the project.

Mission

To make the most realistic, most authentic jousting game ever made: the joust as it was, built from real footage, real rules, real armour and real history.

Jousting was the great spectator sport of late-medieval Europe, and it still has no game that takes it seriously. Football has FIFA. Jousting deserves its own definitive sports game: one that gets the rules, the kit, the horses and the moment of the strike right, and is fun to play for a minute or a whole season.

That is the job. Every part of the game starts from evidence: the ordinances the heralds scored by, the tournaments that really took place, the knights who rode in them, armour of the period, horses at their true size, and the way a lance and a rider really behave, measured from footage of real jousts.

Vision: where it is going

  1. Now · The free browser game

    Play on phone or desktop, free: five real venues, eight rule sets, nine real opponents, a career of tournaments, online play and ranked jousts. Live

  2. The PC version

    A full PC game with top-tier graphics: real faces, full-body motion, armour modelled from museum pieces, crowds in their thousands. An Unreal Engine version is planned, and the browser game is where the joust is being proven first. Planned

  3. Leagues and seasons

    Ranked play with Glicko-2 ratings and divisions is live today. Ranked seasons with promotion and relegation, and leagues with friends, are next on the online side. Coming

  4. A community around the sport

    A home for players, and for the people who know the joust best: historians, the historical martial arts (HEMA) community, and the riders of today's professional jousting circuit. We want their eyes on the game and their corrections in it. Planned

See the full roadmap for what is live, in development and planned.

Why support now

Joust Revival is made by a very small independent team. There is no publisher and no investor behind it, and the game is free.

The best way to support us right now is to play, share and give feedback. Every clip you share and every thing you tell us felt wrong helps the game get built sooner and better, and you get a say while it is still being shaped.

Ways to support the game

What we believe

  • Fair play. No pay-to-win in ranked. Ranked is decided on the lance: you win on your riding, your aim and your timing.
  • Historical accuracy. Real rules, real venues, real people, real kit. When we take a liberty for the sake of the game, we say so, and we credit our sources.
  • Respect for the real sport. Jousting is still ridden today, by riders who train for years and take real risks. We learn from their footage and want to do justice to what they do.
