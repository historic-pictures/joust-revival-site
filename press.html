In short

One line: Joust Revival is a jousting game built from the real thing: real rules, real venues, real knights, and motion measured from real footage.

One paragraph: Joust Revival puts you in the lists of the great tournaments of the 14th and 15th centuries, under the rules they were really run by, from Tiptoft's Ordinances of 1466 to the German Gestech. You ride against real jousters, such as Boucicaut, Jacques de Lalaing and Suero de Quiñones, in four period harnesses on four breeds of horse at their true size. The horses' gallop is matched to Muybridge's 1878 photographs, and the physics is being tuned to measurements from twenty real jousting videos. Joust Revival is free to play in the browser on phone and desktop, with online and ranked play. A full PC version in Unreal Engine is in development.

Key features

  • Five real venues, eight rule sets, nine real opponents
  • Four harnesses (c. 1250 to c. 1500), four helms, three lances, four horse breeds
  • Splintering lance breaks, falls, injuries and the surgeon
  • Replays from five angles, visor views, free camera, slow motion
  • Online: friends, public rooms, ranked with Glicko-2 and six divisions
