Who we are

Joust Revival is an independent game. The operator of this website and the game is [NAME AND ADDRESS TO BE ADDED]. Contact: [CONTACT E-MAIL TO BE ADDED].

This website

  • The website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and loads no third-party scripts.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to run the service. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Newsletter (when it opens): if you sign up, we store your e-mail address with our newsletter provider [PROVIDER TO BE ADDED] to send you the Herald's Letter. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every e-mail.

The game

  • On your device: the game saves your progress and settings in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device unless you sign in.
  • Accounts: if you create an account, we store your player name, your sign-in details and your saved progress on our server so you can play on other devices.
  • Online and ranked play: to run matches and rankings we process your player name, a player identifier, match results and your rating. Your name and rating can appear on the public leaderboard.
  • Playing with others: online matches connect your browser directly to your opponent's (peer to peer), so your opponent's device can see your IP address, as with most online games. Public rooms are announced through public relays and contain only the room code, your chosen room name and the match settings.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Depending on where you live (for example in the UK or EU), you may also complain to your data protection authority.
