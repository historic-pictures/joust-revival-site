Free ways to help

  • Play, and tell us what you think. The browser game is where the joust gets tuned. What felt wrong is as useful as what felt right.
  • Share a clip. A broken lance in slow motion travels further than any advert.
  • Sign up for the Herald's Letter at the bottom of this page, so you hear when the Steam page opens.
  • Wishlist the PC version when the Steam page goes up. Wishlists are how a small game gets seen on Steam.
